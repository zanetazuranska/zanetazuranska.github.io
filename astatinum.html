Astatinum

Astatinum is a story-driven game created for the “Turniej Trójgamiczny” competition, where our project ranked in the top 10 teams.

The game tells the story of a young girl living in a post-war world, trying to save her ill grandfather. His heart is supported by a special device that requires astatinum — an extremely rare and hard-to-obtain element. Faced with this challenge, she sets out alone on a dangerous journey to find it.

Development Notes ▾

Project Challenges

This was our first large-scale team project combining programming, art, and narrative design. We decided to create a story-driven game, which required building not only gameplay systems, but also the entire narrative structure from scratch.

  • Designing and integrating a dialogue system that could properly fit into gameplay flow.
  • Synchronizing FMOD audio events with animation states in a consistent way.
  • Managing a 10-person team as a first-time team leader, including task distribution and progress tracking.
  • Learning how to structure a game architecture without prior experience, mainly through tutorials and peer learning.

How We Solved It

We approached the project iteratively, learning and improving the structure step by step as the production progressed.

  • Built a modular dialogue system using Ink for better scalability and integration.
  • Implemented event-based communication between Animator and FMOD systems.
  • Introduced Trello as the main project management tool to organize tasks and responsibilities across the team.
  • Developed architecture collaboratively through experimentation, tutorials, and shared learning.

What I would do differently

With the experience I have now, I would choose a less complex first project in scope, especially for a team of this size. The ambition of the project was high, which made the production process more challenging than expected.

However, in hindsight, this "too ambitious" approach turned out to be extremely valuable. It forced us to learn quickly, solve real production problems under pressure, and develop both technical and organizational skills in a very short time.
