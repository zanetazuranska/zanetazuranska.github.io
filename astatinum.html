Astatinum

Astatinum is a role-playing game we made for the “Turniej Trójgamiczny”. Our project ended up in the top ten.

The game tells the story of a young girl living in a post-war world. Her grandfather's heart only beats thanks to a special device, but the problem is that it runs on astatinum. That element is nearly impossible to find. Faced with this challenge, the girl sets out alone on a dangerous journey to find him.

Development Notes ▾

Project Challenges

This was our first big team project where we brought together programming, art, and narrative design. We decided to create a story-driven game, so it wasn`t just about making gameplay systems but also the entire narrative structure.

  • Designing a dialogue system that really fit into gameplay flow.
  • Synchronizing FMOD audio events to match animations.
  • Managing a team of 10 people as a leader for the first time, including task distribution and progress tracking.
  • Learning how to create game architecture without prior experience, mainly through tutorials and peer learning.

How We Solved It

We worked in steps, improving things as we went.

  • Built a modular system using Ink, which made it easy to scale and fit new story parts.
  • Developed an audio management system based on FMOD.
  • We used Trello as our main project management tool to organize tasks and responsibilities.
  • We just experimented, learned as a team, and followed the tutorials.

What I would do differently

Looking back, I`d probably choose something a little less overwhelming for a first project, especially with a team this big. The ambition of the project was high and it made the process more challenging than we expected.

But honestly, our ambitious approach forced us to learn fast. We solved real production problems under pressure and developed both our technical and teamwork skills.
