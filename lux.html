Lux & Morte

Lux & Morte is a fast-paced party game where two players face off in afterlife inspired by Día de los Muertos. You get local multiplayer and there is no time to wait, you have to act.

One player steps in as the Light, navigating maze in an attempt to survive. The other player controls a hunting horde of skulls, It`s all about making fast decisions while trying to outsmart your opponent.

During development, the project was regularly reviewed during evaluation sessions with professionals. That constant loop of playtesting helped improve gameplay mechanics and the overall player experience.

Development Notes ▾

Project Challenges

Making Lux & Morte wasn`t easy, especially since the roles are unequal. One player play as the Light, the other controls the ghosts. Balancing that, and getting local multiplayer to feel right, took some work.

  • Balancing asymmetric gameplay between different player roles.
  • Iterative game balancing through playtest sessions with industry experts.
  • Built a system for switching between different ghosts and making sure players always felt in control.
  • Optimizing Unity Tilemap performance after noticing FPS drops.

How We Solved It

The game was improved through continuous iteration by showing it at public events, collecting feedback, and implementing fixes.

  • Ran frequent playtests with industry, then dialed in movement speed abilities, and overall pacing of asymmetric gameplay.
  • Tried out different ideas for swapping between ghosts.
  • Optimized level performance by reducing Tilemap complexity.

What I Would Do Differently

Looking back, there`s no single “aha” moment. The project got better becouse of lots of little changes, all thanks to showing it off and listening to players. If I could change anything, I`d add performance testing and balance adjustments earlier, especially for heavy Tilemap scenes, to avoid last-minute optimization work. Overall, it`s been an amazing lesson in balancing gameplay.

Events & Showcases ▾

University Industry Showcase - Early Playtest Sessions

2025

One of our first big milestones was getting Lux & Morte in front of real industry eyes. At a university showcase, specialists from studios like CD PROJEKT RED, 11 bit studios, Cleversan Games, and Render Cube tried out the game and give feedback. That first batch of outside opinions showed us what worked and what needed to change.

Bit Boat Festival

2026

At Bit Boat Festival, we put the game in a live public environment, where players could test it.

The event included a community tournament with sponsored prizes, which provided valuable feedback on gameplay balance and player behavior.

Game Access Conference '25

Brno, Czech Republic | 2026

We presented Lux & Morte at Game Access Conference '25 in Brno, which combined both a professional conference and an indie showcase environment. It was a chance to dig into current trends, trade stories with other devs, and most importantly watch players interact with the game in real time.

More information about the showcase can be found here: Lux & Morte - Game Access Indie Showcase page
