Kahun - Custom 3D Engine (C++ / OpenGL)

Kahun is a hex-based action-adventure game inspired by Polynesian and Tiki culture, developed entirely on our custom C++ / OpenGL game engine.

Players take on the role of a young shaman who wields magical runes to battle hostile creatures, explore a mysterious island one hex at a time, and rescue a kidnapped son from the forces that threaten the world.

The project combines tactical hex-based exploration, rune-based combat, and environmental puzzles, while serving as a showcase of the capabilities of our in-house 3D engine.

Project Context

Kahun is an original game developed on our own custom 3D engine written in C++ using OpenGL. The project combines engine development with gameplay production, allowing us to design both low-level technology and game systems in parallel.

Working on both the engine and the game at the same time required close collaboration between programmers and artists, with a strong focus on creating reusable engine systems that could directly support gameplay features.
